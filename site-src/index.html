Sources. Congress: FEC bulk files (candidate summaries, committee-to-candidate contributions, individual contributions, operating expenditures) for the 2025–26 cycle. Congressional donor amounts add up each person's itemized gifts to one campaign and do not subtract refunds. Governor, lieutenant governor, legislature, PACs and party committees: Pennsylvania Department of State full campaign finance export dated November 6, 2026, covering regular reports through the October 19 pre-election filing. District and ZIP code to county matching: U.S. Census Bureau 2020 relationship files.

News. Headlines come from the public RSS feeds of about 50 Pennsylvania outlets, checked automatically about every 30 minutes, and are matched to counties by county, town and township names, so a few matches will be wrong. Where a county has fewer than three stories of its own, the panel adds stories from nearby counties and statewide outlets, marked Regional story or Statewide story.

Trending. Issues are counted from the same local feeds plus 12 opinion pages. Tone tags are shown only where a story or letter shows residents reacting, plus 108 older items found by a web search of all 67 counties (56 had findings; items date from late 2025 through 2026, and summaries are written from headlines and excerpts). Web findings skew toward complaints, because that is what public meetings and the reporting on them mostly record. Social media is not included. This is a reading of coverage, not a poll.

Use limits. Federal law bars using individual contributor information from FEC reports to solicit contributions or for commercial purposes.

What is missing. County and municipal candidates file with their county election office, so they are not here. Nothing after the October pre-election reports is in yet; post-election reports are due December 3, 2026. State cash on hand is an estimate from each filer's latest report. Committees are placed in the county of their mailing address. Totals add up every filer's receipts, so money a PAC passes to a campaign is counted at both. News and Trending refresh automatically. Campaign money is a snapshot built November 17, 2026.
